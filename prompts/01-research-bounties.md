# Research the Bounties

Use with a deep research tool (Claude Research, ChatGPT Deep Research, Perplexity). One run per bounty, or all at once if they fit.

```
I'm competing in a [24/48]-hour hackathon. Below are the bounty briefs and judging rubrics.

For each bounty, research and report:
1. The sponsor: what they sell, who their customers are, and what problem this bounty solves for them
2. What already exists: competitors, open-source projects, prior hackathon winners for similar problems
3. The data: every data source the solution needs, whether it has a public API, auth requirements, rate limits, and a link to the docs. Flag any data that would require scraping.
4. The hard part: the one thing that separates a winning solution from a search box or a CRUD app
5. Domain knowledge a builder needs to avoid being subtly wrong

My background: [your skills, domains you know, stack you're fast in].

Finish with a ranked recommendation of which bounty fits my background best and why, and which to avoid.

[paste briefs and rubrics]
```
